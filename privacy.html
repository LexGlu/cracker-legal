Cracker — Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Cracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a nutrition-logging app for iOS published by Oleksandr Glushchenko. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. It applies to the Cracker iOS app and its backend services.

Data we collect

Account data. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we store your account identifier, email address, and authentication session — handled by our self-hosted authentication service, Zitadel. Account and application data are stored in PostgreSQL on Google Cloud infrastructure.

Health data (Apple Health). With your permission, the app reads your sex, date of birth, height, weight, steps, workouts, sleep, and heart rate from Apple Health so your calorie and macro targets reflect your body and activity. This data is used on-device and on our servers to calculate your targets, show activity and progress, and produce requested insights — it is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Separately, and only if you turn it on in Settings, the app can write the meals you log back to Apple Health as food entries (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar, sodium, potassium, and saturated fat), so other apps you use can see what you ate. Reading and writing Health data are independent permissions you grant separately, and either can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Meals you log. We process photos, voice recordings, barcodes and text you submit, together with the resulting meal and nutrition records. Meal photos are stored in Google Cloud Storage. Unreferenced photos are removed by scheduled cleanup after a grace period, rather than immediately when a meal is removed. Voice recordings are processed for transcription and are not saved as server-side meal attachments. Pending captures may remain on your device until completed or discarded.

Optional AI processing. Before AI processing, the app asks you to allow the disclosed recipients to process selected meal text, photos and audio, and relevant nutrition and health context used for requested insights. The current AI recipient is Google Gemini. These requests recognize food, estimate nutrition, transcribe speech and generate nutrition insights. The backend may choose a model automatically within the disclosed provider list. A new recipient requires renewed acceptance before that recipient can receive your data. You can withdraw permission under Account → AI and your data. This stops new AI requests but cannot recall data already sent. Manual logging, saved meals and barcode lookup remain available without AI permission. Provider processing is subject to its applicable service and privacy terms.

Subscription and purchase data. If you subscribe to Cracker Premium, Apple processes your payment — we never see your card details. Purchase receipts and your subscription status are managed through RevenueCat, which receives your in-app user identifier and purchase/transaction data from Apple to determine your entitlement.

Usage and diagnostics. We log API requests (endpoint, timestamp, response status, and account identifier) for reliability, abuse prevention, and to enforce fair-use limits on AI-powered features. PostHog receives usage events and account identifiers for product analytics. Sentry receives crash and performance diagnostics and account identifiers for troubleshooting. We do not use advertising trackers or send health records, meal content, photos or voice recordings as analytics event properties. Telemetry is filtered to reduce sensitive content.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app's core functionality: logging meals, computing nutrition, and tracking progress toward your goals.
  • To calculate personalised calorie and macro targets from your health profile.
  • To manage your account, subscription, and entitlements.
  • To maintain and improve service reliability and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health data or meal photos for advertising.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers necessary to run the app, each acting as our data processor under its own privacy terms:

  • Zitadel (self-hosted) — account authentication.
  • Google Cloud Platform — file storage and infrastructure hosting.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI analysis of meal photos, voice, and text.
  • Apple — sign-in, HealthKit, and in-app purchase processing.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • PostHog — product usage analytics.
  • Sentry — crash and performance diagnostics.

We do not share your data with data brokers or advertisers, and we disclose data to law enforcement only when legally required to do so.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data and meal history are retained for as long as your account is active. Request deletion in Account → Delete account. Access is suspended while the recoverable deletion window is open; scheduled cleanup then permanently removes the account and associated application data. You may also contact us for deletion help. Backup copies age out under their retention policies. Some records may be retained where required by law. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it separately in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for optional permissions (such as Health data access) at any time. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Children's privacy

Cracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
